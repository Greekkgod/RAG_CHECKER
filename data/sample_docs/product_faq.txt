Product FAQ: CloudSync Pro

What is the data retention policy?
CloudSync Pro retains deleted files in the Trash for 30 days before permanent deletion.
Files deleted from Trash manually are removed immediately and cannot be recovered.
Enterprise plan customers get an extended retention window of 90 days.

What are the storage limits?
The Free plan includes 5 GB of storage. The Pro plan includes 200 GB. The Enterprise plan
includes unlimited storage but is subject to a fair-use cap of 5 TB per user per month, beyond
which additional charges apply at 2 USD per 100 GB.

How does file versioning work?
CloudSync Pro keeps the last 25 versions of any file for Free and Pro plans. Enterprise plans
keep unlimited version history. Version history older than 180 days is automatically pruned
for Free plan users only.

What is the maximum file upload size?
Individual file uploads are capped at 5 GB for Free and Pro plans, and 50 GB for Enterprise
plans. Files larger than this must be uploaded using the CloudSync CLI tool with chunked upload
enabled.

Is two-factor authentication supported?
Yes. Two-factor authentication (2FA) via authenticator app is available on all plans. SMS-based
2FA is only available on Pro and Enterprise plans due to per-message carrier costs.

What happens if I downgrade my plan?
If your used storage exceeds the limit of your new (lower) plan, your account is placed in
read-only mode until you either delete files to fit within the new limit or upgrade again.
You will not lose any data during this read-only period, but you cannot upload new files.
