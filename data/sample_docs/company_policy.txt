Remote Work Policy

Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Full-time remote work requires written approval from both the manager and HR, and is only granted
for roles classified as "remote-eligible" in the HR system.

Employees working remotely must be available during core hours of 10 AM to 4 PM local time.
Internet reimbursement of up to 1500 INR per month is provided for employees who work remotely
at least 2 days per week.

Equipment provided for remote work includes a laptop and a monitor. Employees must return all
company equipment within 5 business days of resignation or termination.

Leave Policy

Employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month, up to a maximum of 18 days per year.
Unused leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year, up to a cap of 10 days.
Sick leave is separate from paid leave and is capped at 12 days per year, requiring a medical
certificate for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks as per statutory requirements. Paternity leave is 2 weeks.

Expense Reimbursement Policy

All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred, along with
original receipts. Claims submitted after 30 days require VP-level approval to be processed.

Travel expenses for client visits are reimbursed at actual cost, provided they fall within the
pre-approved travel budget for that client engagement. Per diem for domestic travel is 1200 INR
per day; international per diem is 60 USD per day.

Reimbursements are processed within 15 business days of approval and are credited directly to
the employee's registered bank account.
